What we do

Three causes, one mission

Every rupee donated goes directly into one of the three programs below.

📘

Education

School fees, books, and uniforms for children whose families can't otherwise afford to keep them enrolled.

🩺

Health

Free medical camps, basic medicines, and referrals for families with no easy access to a doctor.

🤝

Poverty Relief

Ration support and small emergency grants for households going through a sudden hard patch.

Our story

Built to help, kept simple on purpose.

Mohsin Welfare Foundation started with a simple idea: help should be direct, transparent, and close to the ground. We work with local volunteers who know their neighborhoods, so support reaches the people who need it most — without layers of bureaucracy in between.

Our promise

Every donation, accounted for.

We believe trust is earned by showing exactly where the money goes — not just asking people to believe it. As we grow, our aim is to publish clear, regular updates on how each program is spending its funds.
